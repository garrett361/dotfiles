# Grill A Plan

Interview the user relentlessly about the plan until the major decisions are resolved.

## Workflow

1. Explore the codebase first for any facts you can discover directly.
2. Ask one question at a time for the highest-impact unresolved decision.
3. For each question, include your recommended answer.
4. Use the user's answer to choose the next branch of the design tree.
5. Continue until the plan is concrete enough to implement without guesswork.

## Rules

- Prefer discovering repo facts over asking about them.
- Ask about product intent, constraints, and tradeoffs early.
- Keep each question concise and decision-relevant.
- Do not dump a long questionnaire all at once.
